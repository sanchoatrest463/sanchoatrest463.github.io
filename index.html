About This Tool

This software helps you save YouTube content to your hard drive. You can download entire playlists or single videos. It supports video files in mp4 format and audio files in mp3 format. You can also pick specific start and end times if you only need a portion of a video.

🛠️ Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows computer.

  1. Visit the official download page to grab the files.
  2. Locate the downloaded zip folder on your computer.
  3. Right-click the folder and select Extract All to unzip the contents.
  4. Open the extracted folder.
  5. Double-click the main executable file to start the program.

💻 System Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • An active internet connection.
  • Enough storage space for your saved files.

💡 How to Download Videos

Once you open the software, you will see a simple window. Copy the URL of the YouTube video or playlist you want to save. Paste the address into the box provided in the app. Choose your desired output format, such as mp4 for video or mp3 for audio. If you want a specific clip, enter the start and end times. Click the Download button to begin the transfer. Wait for the process to finish, and the software will notify you when your files are ready.

⚙️ Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, ensure you have a stable internet connection. Check that you have enough space on your drive for the download. Ensure your web address is correct. This tool relies on yt-dlp to handle media retrieval, which ensures high compatibility with YouTube updates.
